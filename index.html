theRealDyer

I'm James and I'm a hobbyist game developer making hand-crafted games, living in the UK. I'm also a puzzle designer, playtester, rockclimber, musician, and occasional streamer.

All of my work is created the long, hard way, i.e., without generative AI, with a focus on handmade design, writing, and development. And always will be.
