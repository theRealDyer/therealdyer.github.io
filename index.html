I'm James and I'm a hobbyist game developer making hand-crafted games, living in the UK. I also playtest, rockclimb, occasionally stream, and enjoy all sorts of games. All of my work is created the long, hard way, i.e., without generative AI, with a focus on handmade design, writing, and development.
