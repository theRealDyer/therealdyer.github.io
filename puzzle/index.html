Light Up rules:

  • Place bulbs so every white square is lit.
  • Bulbs cannot see each other in a row or column unless blocked by a black square.
  • Numbered black squares must touch exactly that many bulbs.

Controls:

  • Left click to place/remove light bulb.
  • Right click to rule out a space with an "X".
